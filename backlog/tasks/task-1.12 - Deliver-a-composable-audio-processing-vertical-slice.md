#### Overload and latency: mechanism and tails

W accumulates 480 samples (10 ms at 48 kHz), performs synchronous native inference in the callback that completes that frame, then emits its result over the next frame. RNNoise analysis/synthesis alignment is 960 samples (20 ms); staging adds another 480 samples. The corresponding direct framing/alignment contribution is approximately **30 ms**, before graph/media-route buffering. Dry audio is delayed by 960 samples before mixing and shares output staging; it is aligned with wet audio, not an extra independent delay to add again. Removing that delay alone would misalign mixed speech. The comparison in T checks the assumed mixing formula but is not an independent acoustic/group-delay validation; strong/full-wet already incurs the high loopback latency.

The guard times `RnnoiseKernel.processFrame`, including its copies/finite validation, and compares against **output.length/sampleRate**, usually 128/48000 = 2.6667 ms. Two consecutive *model frames* over that threshold trigger permanent fault for that processor, native destroy, temporary same-Worklet dry pass-through and host publication of original input/disposal. These are two model invocations about 10 ms apart, not necessarily adjacent 128-sample callbacks. A 10 ms average inference budget is invalid: computation must fit the callback containing it. Conversely, the current timer excludes downmix/sample loops, mixing, message serialization and shared graph work, and cannot observe scheduling deadlines; isolated large misses may not trigger it. Timer granularity or descheduling can also make a measured pair conservative. Do not loosen the guard to make tests green.

`timing` is posted once per processed model frame (~100/s); host stores at most 1000 timings and reports the length as `frames` (R). These are rolling, delivered-message metrics, not total lifetime frames, callback p95 or audio-path p95. The timer fallback/quantization requires calibration; current CI Chromium p50=0 ms with nonzero processing is direct evidence that those numbers cannot establish sub-ms headroom.

Eight processed windows from one repeated speech loop are correlated, not eight independent device trials or thousands of independent latency samples. Low correlation/silence and >300 ms stalls can disappear from accepted estimates: retain exclusion counts and outages rather than report only surviving windows. 3:1 downsampling lacks an explicit anti-alias filter; use a bandlimited pilot or correctly resample in the next harness.

#### Concrete remediation alternatives and acceptance measurements
